What is an opinion essay?

You answer a question with your opinion, give reasons, and explain them with examples.

חיבור דעה: אומרים מה אני חושב, ונותנים סיבות ודוגמאות

📓 Notebook · Task 1

Copy the 4 parts from the next slide.

The 4 parts

1 · Introduction

Repeat the question in your words + your opinion.

2 · Reason 1

First reason + example.

3 · Reason 2

Second reason + example.

4 · Conclusion

Say your opinion again in different words.

Useful phrases

Opinion

In my opinion, … לדעתי
I believe that … אני מאמין ש
I (dis)agree that … אני (לא) מסכים

Reasons

First of all, … קודם כל
Moreover, … יתרה מזאת
Another reason is … סיבה נוספת

Examples

For example, … לדוגמה
For instance, … למשל

Conclusion

In conclusion, … לסיכום
To sum up, … בסיכומו של דבר

Model essay · Topic 17

At what age should children be allowed to have their own cellphone?

Many parents ask when their children should get a cellphone. In my opinion, children should get their own phone at the age of 12.
First of all, at 12 many children go to school and to activities alone. For example, a phone helps them call their parents if the bus is late.
Moreover, at this age children are more responsible. They can follow rules, like not using the phone at night.
In conclusion, I believe 12 is the right age, because children need a phone to be safe and they are old enough to use it well.
